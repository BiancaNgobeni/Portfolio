02 / Project Context

Understanding the Problem.

Educational reporting across juvenile facilities relied heavily on spreadsheets and manually prepared documents. Educators needed to capture assessment results, calculate marks, compile schedules and prepare student reports.

These activities were repetitive and could lead to inconsistencies in the management of academic information. Our team developed the Educational Reporting System for the Western Cape Government Department of Social Development to centralise educational information and simplify reporting.

The Challenge

Disconnected records, repetitive manual processes, inconsistent reporting and difficulties managing educational information.

The Proposed Solution

A centralised platform for student information, mark capture, academic setup, reporting and role-based access.

08 / Reflection

What I Took from the Experience.

Working on ERPS strengthened my understanding of how Business Analysis connects requirements, planning, system design and software development. It gave me experience contributing to a solution addressing an existing organisational problem.

Designing the interface also reinforced the importance of creating systems that are practical and accessible for their intended users. Working with a team required clear communication, flexibility and shared responsibility for delivering the project.

More importantly, the experience reminded me that developing a system is not only about making something work. It is about understanding the people who will use it and building something that addresses a real need.
