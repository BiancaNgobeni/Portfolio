01

The Context

Township communities have local businesses, informal traders and service providers offering a wide range of products and services. However, discovering these businesses or finding reliable information about available services is not always straightforward.

Kasi 411 is an independent project exploring a digital space where residents could find local businesses, discover services and access community information in one place.

02

The Problem

How can local businesses become easier to discover?

The problem Kasi 411 aims to address is fragmented access to information about local products, businesses and services. Residents may depend on word of mouth or scattered online information, while smaller businesses may have limited digital visibility.

DESIGN CHALLENGE

Create a simple, accessible way for residents to discover what is available in their communities, while giving local businesses greater visibility.

03

Proposed Solution

Kasi 411 is envisioned as a hyperlocal e-commerce and community information platform connecting residents with businesses and service providers in their area.
